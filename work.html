सवाल और जवाब // QUESTIONS

Frequently
asked questions

What kind of clients and projects do you work with?

We collaborate with artists, stand-up comedians, actors, healthcare pioneers, podcasts, and leading consumer brands (such as Traya Health, mCaffeine, Kibo Hair Science, and Pronto). We support their creative journey through video editing, social media management, brand PR, poster typography, and digital content.

Do you handle both short-form and long-form video editing?

Yes, absolutely. We produce long-form YouTube episodes and podcast recordings with multi-cam switching and deep audio mastering, as well as fast-paced, high-retention short-form video (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikToks) engineered for maximum organic reach and algorithmic growth.

How do you manage social media and PR for talent & artists?

For personalities like Sushmita Mukherjee and Dr. Kumar Vishwas, we provide full-service social media management: content scheduling, copy crafting, brand partnership alignment, and strategic event publicity to reinforce credibility and cultural authority.

What is your turnaround time for creative deliverables?

Turnaround depends on the scope. For ongoing creator partnerships (reels, thumbnails, weekly YouTube cuts), turnarounds are typically 24 to 48 hours. For tour campaign posters or major brand launches, we work in fast sprints with structured review cycles.

Can you design promotional posters and physical campaign artwork?

Yes. Our work on projects like the Halwa Tour Poster and Rock Paper Rum demonstrates our deep commitment to typography, visual hierarchy, and distinctive artwork design that functions seamlessly across both digital feeds and high-format physical print.

How do we start a collaboration with 4—11 Studio?

Click "Start a project" or "Book a call" to tell us about your channel, brand, or project. We'll set up an initial discovery session to outline creative direction, deliverables, and timeline.
