Most pitch decks fail before the Q&A. Here's why.

A founder once told us they spent $8,000 on a pitch deck that looked beautiful and raised nothing. The design was polished. The narrative wasn't. The slides didn't answer the questions a VC has in the first three minutes. The traction slide buried the number that mattered.

Design without strategy is decoration. A pitch deck isn't a brochure : it's a structured argument with a visual language that makes that argument credible and memorable. The decks that advance are rarely the prettiest : they're the ones where the narrative is airtight and the design amplifies the signal.

Frequently
asked questions

Do you write the content or just design it?

Both, in sequence. We start with narrative strategy : helping structure and sharpen the argument : before any slide design begins. If you already have a strong narrative locked, we can take it from there and focus on design and visualization. If you're starting from a rough deck or an idea, we work through the structure with you first.

How is this different from a specialized pitch deck agency?

Pitch deck factories produce beautiful slides. We produce a coherent fundraising document that is an extension of your brand, your product, and your narrative : not a standalone deliverable designed by someone who has never seen your actual product. For high-ticket raises where brand credibility matters, that coherence is the difference.

What do investors actually care about in a deck?

In the first three slides: problem clarity, solution credibility, and market size. After that: traction (evidence of momentum), team (evidence of execution), and ask (clarity of what you need and why). We design each slide to answer the specific question a VC is asking at that moment in the reading sequence.

Can you work on just the design if we already have the narrative?

Yes. If your narrative is solid and you need Figma-native execution, we can scope a design-only engagement. We'll still review the slide structure during onboarding and flag anything that creates unnecessary friction for an investor reader : even if you don't ask us to.

Do you design presentations other than pitch decks?

Yes. Beyond fundraising decks, we design sales decks, board and investor update templates, conference keynotes, and partnership presentations. Most clients ask for a master template in Google Slides or PowerPoint as well, so the team can produce the next ten presentations on-brand without coming back to us.

Do you include financial projection slides?

Yes. We design the layout and visualization for financial slides. We don't create or validate the underlying financial model : that's your CFO or financial advisor's domain. We make those numbers legible, credible, and instantly comprehensible.
